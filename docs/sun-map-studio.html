How to read it

In Live shadow the colour is the instantaneous answer — gold where the sun reaches the floor right now, blue where something blocks it. Scrub the time bar to step through the day and watch the shaded band sweep. In Sun-hours each cell is coloured by its total daylight hours across the whole day — that integrated map is what the plant matcher actually reads.

The balcony shows why the slab overhead matters: the back half sits in soffit shade most of the day while the open rail catches the low morning and evening sun. The garden's story is the house and the tree; the plot's is the treeline to the north. Same Space model, same shadow projection — only the boundary, the obstacles, and the cell size change.
